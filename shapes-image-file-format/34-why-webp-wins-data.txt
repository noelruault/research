MATCHED-FIDELITY COMPARISON — affine loses everywhere:

  ~26.3-26.6 dB   constant  4619 B      affine  5500 B
  ~27.0-27.4 dB   constant  6802 B      affine  8900 B
  ~28.1-28.4 dB   constant 11057 B      affine 15500 B
  ~29.2-29.4 dB   constant 14356 B      affine 27300 B   (1.9x)

MECHANISM: affine DOES deliver what it promised on geometry. 1,024 affine regions reach 28.06 dB
with 29,145 crack edges where the constant arm needs 41,580 crack edges. But the plane
coefficients cost 9.2 KB against the boundary's 6.3 KB. Nine numbers per region instead of three
costs more than the walls it saves.

FAIRNESS OBJECTION, AND WHY THE CONCLUSION SURVIVES IT: affine's plane coder predates RCT, so it
is measured with the weaker colour coder while the constant arm has the stronger one -- exactly
the trap PARKED.md warns about. Grant affine a generous RCT-scale 28% discount on planes:
9.2 KB -> 6.6 KB, total 12.9 KB at 28.06 dB. Constant is 11057 B at the HIGHER 28.35 dB. Still
worse. The conclusion does not depend on the unfairness.

This DOWNGRADES rather than formally closes P-08: a proper close would re-measure the plane
coefficients through RCT rather than discount them arithmetically.

P-07 is the only lever of the right size, and it costs the property being sold: report 28's
finding is that a STABLE, EXACT partition is what a re-segmenting client cannot reproduce. A
lossy boundary is a different product and should be entered deliberately, not as a byte tweak.
